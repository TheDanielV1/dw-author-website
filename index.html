Daniel Vendramini

Author | Student | Triplet

Daniel Vendramini is an up-and-coming writer from Melbourne, Victoria. Currently studying a Bachelor of Arts in Creative Writing, Daniels work primarily consistent of fantasy fiction prose, but with his practice still in its early infancy continues to experiment with different forms and genres. His method of writing often sees him listening to music to enter a creative flow, and in his downtime is constantly consuming media of all forms, from early 2000’s serials to classic literature or Korean light novels.

From a young age, Daniel has believed vehemently in the power held in stories, and through his writing desires to continue the magic of storytelling for younger generations to experience, like he once did. Fun Fact - Daniel is a triplet.
